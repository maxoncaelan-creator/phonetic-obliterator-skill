# Style registry

Each style changes **word choice and tone only**. It never changes the phoneme budget, the locked phrases or the facts. A style has to fit inside the same budget, so pick short, vivid words over added length.

Each entry has:
- **Voice:** what the style sounds like.
- **Do:** word choices to reach for.
- **Avoid:** what breaks the style.
- **Sample:** an illustrative line from the "fly under a lampshade" text. Samples aren't phoneme-checked, so show the feel and don't copy them.

`auto` isn't an entry here. It picks one of these, or records a new one (see SKILL.md, "Auto style").

---

## none
- **Voice:** the original author's own voice, just shorter. No new tone.
- **Do:** keep the author's favourite words, punctuation habits and emoji. Cut filler only.
- **Avoid:** new jokes, metaphors or attitude.
- **Sample:** "It's not lost or drawn to light!"

## clean
- **Voice:** plain, clear and neutral, like a good encyclopedia caption.
- **Do:** short common words, one idea per sentence, active verbs, no exclamation marks.
- **Avoid:** slang, emoji in the body, rhetorical questions, hype.
- **Sample:** "These are male lesser houseflies. They circle to find a mate."

## corporate
- **Voice:** a polished brand or LinkedIn post: confident, upbeat, value-led.
- **Do:** crisp "insight" framing ("Here's the thing:", "Key takeaway:"), terms like "strategy", "focus", "show up". Keep the jargon short.
- **Avoid:** long buzzword chains (they cost phonemes), slang, anything risqué.
- **Sample:** "Key takeaway: he shows up, lights on or off. That's focus."

## moody
- **Voice:** quiet, wistful and dusky, like late-night journaling.
- **Do:** words for dark, hush, alone and waiting. Ellipses and short fragments, with soft endings.
- **Avoid:** exclamation marks, jokes, bright cheer.
- **Sample:** "He circles in the dark… still waiting for her."

## emo-metal
- **Voice:** dramatic, anguished and loud, like song lyrics screamed into the void. It's tongue-in-cheek and over the top.
- **Do:** words like doom, void, ache, eternal, scream and darkness. ALL-CAPS bursts are fine in small doses. Cast the fly as a tragic hero.
- **Avoid:** cosy words, real self-harm imagery, slurs.
- **Sample:** "He circles the void, eternal, for a love that may never come."

## lusty
- **Voice:** flirty, sultry and full of innuendo, like a wink across a candlelit room. Suggestive, **never explicit**.
- **Do:** words like seduce, swoon, smoulder, hunk, lady, come hither. Double meanings and playful heat.
- **Avoid:** graphic sexual content, crude anatomy, anything involving minors. Keep it safe for a general social feed.
- **Sample:** "He's a lesser housefly lothario, waltzing till his lady swoons by."

## whimsy
- **Voice:** playful storybook wonder, gently silly.
- **Do:** twirls, waltzes, dizzy, little bachelor, a lady drops by. Personify the subject.
- **Avoid:** sarcasm, gloom, technical terms beyond the locks.
- **Sample:** "He's doing his dizzy love waltz till a lady drops by."

# Tone and mood

## deadpan
- **Voice:** dry, flat and understated. The joke is how little fuss it makes.
- **Do:** plain statements, full stops, sly understatement ("Romance. Under a lamp.").
- **Avoid:** exclamation marks, emoji in the body, gushing.
- **Sample:** "He isn't lost. He's dating."

## sarcastic
- **Voice:** eye-rolling and snarky, but affectionate rather than cruel.
- **Do:** "oh sure", "naturally", "because of course", mock surprise.
- **Avoid:** mean-spiritedness toward readers, insults about real people.
- **Sample:** "Lost? Please. He's on a date, obviously."

## wholesome
- **Voice:** warm, gentle and feel-good, like a kind neighbour.
- **Do:** sweet, little, hope, heart, cosy, kind.
- **Avoid:** irony, edge, darkness.
- **Sample:** "He's just a little guy, hoping to find love."

## cosy-naturalist
- **Voice:** a fireside nature diary that's curious, tender and observant.
- **Do:** notice, little, wild, homely, the quiet wonders of the house.
- **Avoid:** hype, jargon beyond the locks, cynicism.
- **Sample:** "Watch him circle the lamp, a small wild thing courting at home."

## gothic
- **Voice:** candlelit, ornate and mournful. A Victorian ghost writing letters.
- **Do:** shadow, candle, sorrow, vigil, crypt-cool imagery.
- **Avoid:** modern slang, cheer.
- **Sample:** "Beneath the shade he keeps his vigil, a pale suitor in shadow."

## noir
- **Voice:** hard-boiled detective narration in the first person.
- **Do:** clipped similes, rain, dames, trouble, "the kind of…".
- **Avoid:** softness, emoji, long clauses.
- **Sample:** "He worked the lamp beat. Waiting on a dame. Lights out, he stayed."

## zen
- **Voice:** calm, spare and mindful, close to haiku prose.
- **Do:** breathe, be, still, simply, present tense.
- **Avoid:** exclamation marks, urgency, clutter.
- **Sample:** "He circles. He waits. Light or dark, he simply is."

## hype
- **Voice:** maximum energy, like a pep rally.
- **Do:** ALL-CAPS bursts, "LET'S GO", exclamation marks, fire emoji at the line ends.
- **Avoid:** quiet nuance, long words (caps don't cost phonemes, words do).
- **Sample:** "THIS GUY. Circling NONSTOP. Lights OFF? STILL GOING!"

## melancholy-poet
- **Voice:** tender and elegiac, like a lonely poem.
- **Do:** longing, alone, ache, soft rhythm, gentle line breaks.
- **Avoid:** jokes, hype, bluntness.
- **Sample:** "He circles a lamp for one who may not come."

## unhinged
- **Voice:** chaotic gremlin energy: absurd and joyful.
- **Do:** wild tangents in few words, "absolutely feral", lowercase chaos.
- **Avoid:** cruelty, real-world harm jokes, losing the facts.
- **Sample:** "he's not lost. he's FERAL for love. lights off? doesn't care."

# Genre and fiction

## fairy-tale
- **Voice:** storybook narration with "once upon a time" cadence.
- **Do:** once, little, lady, enchanted, happily, sets of three.
- **Avoid:** modern slang, sarcasm.
- **Sample:** "Once upon a lampshade, a little fly waited for his lady."

## epic-fantasy
- **Voice:** grand and mythic: quests, prophecies and destiny.
- **Do:** quest, realm, sworn, destined, capitalised Titles.
- **Avoid:** casual slang, small stakes.
- **Sample:** "Sworn to the Lamp, he rides his endless quest for a bride."

## sci-fi
- **Voice:** a starship log with cool, techy wonder.
- **Do:** orbit, signal, log entry, sensors, units.
- **Avoid:** fantasy words, gushing.
- **Sample:** "Log: male in fixed orbit under lamp. Awaiting female signal."

## horror
- **Voice:** creeping dread and a suspense build-up, without gore.
- **Do:** something, waiting, in the dark, it never stops.
- **Avoid:** graphic violence, gore, real-world trauma.
- **Sample:** "It circles. It waits. And when the light goes out… it keeps going."

## western
- **Voice:** frontier drawl, campfire yarns.
- **Do:** reckon, partner, varmint, yonder, ain't.
- **Avoid:** offensive caricature, modern tech words.
- **Sample:** "That varmint ain't lost, partner. He's courtin'."

## pirate
- **Voice:** a seafaring rogue: "arr", sea slang, swagger.
- **Do:** arr, matey, ahoy, sails, treasure, lass.
- **Avoid:** overdoing the dialect so much that it can't be read.
- **Sample:** "Arr, he sails 'round the lamp, matey, seekin' a lass!"

## space-opera
- **Voice:** grand, galactic and melodramatic, with empires and stars.
- **Do:** galaxy, empire, star, fate, sweeping lines.
- **Avoid:** mundane framing, small words for big moments.
- **Sample:** "Round his lamp-star he orbits, a lone prince seeking his queen."

## cosmic-horror
- **Voice:** eldritch and unknowable. Strange, ancient things the mind can't grasp.
- **Do:** ancient, unknowable, ritual, beyond, the void.
- **Avoid:** gore, real occult instructions.
- **Sample:** "An ancient ritual, beyond our ken, done in the dark beneath the shade."

## superhero
- **Voice:** comic-book bravado with origin-story energy.
- **Do:** hero, mission, patrol, POW-style bursts, "by day…".
- **Avoid:** copyrighted hero names and catchphrases.
- **Sample:** "By night, he's on patrol, a hero of love, lights or no lights!"

## soap-opera
- **Voice:** gasps, betrayals and dramatic reveals.
- **Do:** "But wait…", shocking, secret, dramatic pauses.
- **Avoid:** flat delivery.
- **Sample:** "He circles… he turns… he's NOT lost. He's waiting for HER."

# Formats and voices

## nature-documentary
- **Voice:** a hushed, reverent wildlife narrator.
- **Do:** "here we see", remarkable, the male, the female, present tense.
- **Avoid:** imitating a specific real narrator by name, slang.
- **Sample:** "Here we see the male, on patrol, awaiting a female."

## sports-commentator
- **Voice:** play-by-play excitement.
- **Do:** "and he's off!", lap, turn, what a move, crowd noise.
- **Avoid:** quiet nuance.
- **Sample:** "And he's off! Lap after lap… a sharp turn… WHAT a move!"

## news-anchor
- **Voice:** a crisp breaking-news bulletin.
- **Do:** "This just in", sources say, officials, the dateline feel.
- **Avoid:** emoji, slang, made-up quotes from real people.
- **Sample:** "This just in: lamp fly not lost. Sources say: mating."

## true-crime
- **Voice:** a podcast cold open: hushed and suspenseful.
- **Do:** "it started", every night, no one knew, the lamp.
- **Avoid:** real crimes and victims, gore.
- **Sample:** "Every night, the same lamp. The same circles. No one knew why."

## infomercial
- **Voice:** a pushy TV pitch, delivered with a wink.
- **Do:** "but wait, there's more!", amazing, now, call.
- **Avoid:** real products, prices or claims that read as a genuine ad.
- **Sample:** "He circles! He waits! But wait, there's more: lights OFF!"

## motivational-speaker
- **Voice:** rise and grind, an inspirational keynote.
- **Do:** you, never quit, show up, your why.
- **Avoid:** medical or financial promises.
- **Sample:** "Lights off? He shows up anyway. Be the fly."

## tour-guide
- **Voice:** a chirpy guided tour.
- **Do:** "on your left", folks, fun fact, step this way.
- **Avoid:** dry exposition.
- **Sample:** "On your left, folks: a fly on a date. Fun fact: lights optional!"

## auctioneer
- **Voice:** rapid-fire selling chant.
- **Do:** repetition, "do I hear", going going gone, quick monosyllables.
- **Avoid:** long words (they kill the rhythm and the budget).
- **Sample:** "Lap one, lap two, do I hear a lady? Going, going… dancing!"

## weather-report
- **Voice:** forecast patter.
- **Do:** expect, scattered, outlook, light conditions, chance of.
- **Avoid:** heavy jargon.
- **Sample:** "Outlook: steady circling, chance of romance, lights on or off."

## field-notes
- **Voice:** a terse scientist's log.
- **Do:** observed, male, female, n., clipped fragments, no emotion.
- **Avoid:** emoji, flourishes, exclamation marks.
- **Sample:** "Observed: male circling lamp. Purpose: mating. Persists in dark."

# Musical and poetic

## haiku-ish
- **Voice:** compressed, seasonal and image-led. It needn't keep strict 5-7-5.
- **Do:** concrete images, line breaks, a turn at the end.
- **Avoid:** explaining, filler words.
- **Sample:** "under the lampshade / he circles, waiting / dark comes; he dances"

## country-ballad
- **Voice:** heartbreak, porches and a slow twang.
- **Do:** lonesome, honey, every night, ol', back porch.
- **Avoid:** quoting real song lyrics.
- **Sample:** "Every night he circles that ol' lamp, waitin' on his honey."

## hip-hop
- **Voice:** rhythm, swagger and internal rhyme.
- **Do:** tight rhymes, flow, flex, confident boasts.
- **Avoid:** slurs, real artists' lyrics, caricature.
- **Sample:** "He loops the light, stays tight, all night, lady in sight."

## sea-shanty
- **Voice:** call-and-response chorus energy.
- **Do:** heave, ho, way-hey, repeated refrains, rolling meter.
- **Avoid:** real copyrighted shanty lines.
- **Sample:** "Way-hey, round he goes, under the lamp he rows!"

## musical-theatre
- **Voice:** a big show tune with a jazz-hands finish.
- **Do:** sung-feel lines, "tonight!", a big final note, stage directions in caps.
- **Avoid:** real show lyrics.
- **Sample:** "Lights on, lights off, he'll dance TONIGHT! (big finish)"

## limerick
- **Voice:** bouncy AABBA rhyme.
- **Do:** anapestic bounce, a silly rhyme, a punchline on the last line.
- **Avoid:** forced meter that hurts the facts.
- **Sample:** "A fly by a lamp, quite a flirt, / Would circle on high, on alert…"

## power-ballad
- **Voice:** soaring 80s drama: hair, wind machine, key change.
- **Do:** forever, heart, burning, the night, held vowels.
- **Avoid:** quoting real songs.
- **Sample:** "Through the dark he'll fly forever, his heart burning for her!"

# Era and dialect

## shakespearean
- **Voice:** Elizabethan flourish.
- **Do:** thee, thou, doth, 'tis, hark, forsooth.
- **Avoid:** using archaic words so densely it can't be read.
- **Sample:** "Hark! He doth circle, not lost, but wooing a lady fair."

## victorian
- **Voice:** prim, florid and earnestly polite.
- **Do:** one observes, a gentleman, most curious, quite.
- **Avoid:** modern slang.
- **Sample:** "One observes a gentleman fly, most curiously courting."

## 1920s-flapper
- **Voice:** jazz-age slang and speakeasy sparkle.
- **Do:** the bee's knees, swell, darling, Charleston, ritzy.
- **Avoid:** offensive period terms.
- **Sample:** "He's doing the Charleston round the lamp, darling, the bee's knees!"

## surfer
- **Voice:** laid-back beach slang.
- **Do:** dude, gnarly, stoked, riding, totally.
- **Avoid:** overload.
- **Sample:** "Dude's totally riding the lamp wave, stoked for a lady."

## aussie-larrikin
- **Voice:** a cheeky, friendly Aussie yarn.
- **Do:** mate, reckon, a bit of a lad, no worries, fair dinkum.
- **Avoid:** crude stereotypes and slurs.
- **Sample:** "He's not lost, mate. Bit of a lad, chasin' a sheila."

## cottagecore
- **Voice:** soft rural romance: gingham, meadows, homemade.
- **Do:** gentle, sweet, meadow, honey, dappled, cottage.
- **Avoid:** cynicism, tech words.
- **Sample:** "A gentle suitor waltzing beneath the cottage lamp."

# Audience and brand

## kids-picture-book
- **Voice:** simple, bouncy wonder for little readers.
- **Do:** short words, repetition, "round and round", friendly questions.
- **Avoid:** innuendo, scary images, hard words beyond the locks.
- **Sample:** "Round and round goes Mister Fly! Who is he waiting for?"

## gen-z
- **Voice:** online, ironic and terminally casual.
- **Do:** lowkey, no cap, it's giving, the way he…, lowercase.
- **Avoid:** dated slang, overuse that reads as cringe.
- **Sample:** "he's not lost, he's lowkey down bad. it's giving romance."

## teacher
- **Voice:** an encouraging classroom explainer.
- **Do:** "Did you know?", let's find out, here's why, simple steps.
- **Avoid:** condescension, jargon.
- **Sample:** "Did you know? He's not lost. Let's find out why he circles!"

## luxury-brand
- **Voice:** hushed, minimal elegance.
- **Do:** spare sentences, refined words, quiet confidence, sentence fragments.
- **Avoid:** exclamation marks, emoji, hype.
- **Sample:** "The lamp. The circle. A ritual, in light or dark."

## wellness-influencer
- **Voice:** glowy, affirming self-care.
- **Do:** intention, energy, honour, you deserve, soft emoji.
- **Avoid:** health claims, shaming.
- **Sample:** "He circles with intention, light or dark. Honour your path. ✨"

## witchy
- **Voice:** moonlit ritual, earthy magic and crystals.
- **Do:** moon, ritual, spell, sacred, wild, circle, charm.
- **Avoid:** real occult instructions, mocking beliefs.
- **Sample:** "His circle is a sacred ritual, cast in light or dark."

## folk-wisdom
- **Voice:** an old-timer's proverb, homespun and knowing.
- **Do:** "as my gran said", old saying, reckon, a lesson.
- **Avoid:** made-up quotes attributed to real people.
- **Sample:** "Old saying: a fly that circles ain't lost, he's waiting on love."
